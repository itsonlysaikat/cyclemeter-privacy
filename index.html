1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CycleMeter ("the application", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Saikat Ray, handles information when you use the CycleMeter mobile application for Android.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. CycleMeter is designed as an offline-first cycling speedometer and ride-tracking tool. We do not require account registration, we do not operate a proprietary cloud backend, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. Information We Access, Process, and Store Locally

A. Device Location and GPS Data

CycleMeter requires access to your device's location services (GPS) to provide core cycling functionality. When an active ride is started, the application accesses fine and coarse location data to calculate:

  • Current real-time speed
  • Average speed and maximum speed
  • Total distance traveled
  • Ride duration (moving time and stopped time)
  • Speed profile graphs over the course of your ride
  • Ride goals and personal records

Local Processing: All GPS readings, route coordinates, and speed calculations are processed and saved locally on your device within the application's private SQLite/Room database. CycleMeter does not transmit your GPS coordinates or route data to any external server, cloud backend, or developer system.

GPS Accuracy Notice: GPS calculations and speed measurements depend entirely on your device's built-in hardware, satellite visibility, weather, physical obstacles, and operating system location providers. Speed and distance readings are provided for informational and athletic tracking purposes only.

B. Local Ride History and User Settings

The following data is created during your use of the application and stored solely on your local device:

  • Completed ride summaries (start time, end time, duration, distance, average speed, max speed, and route point records)
  • Calculated estimated calories burned during a ride
  • User-configured preferences (e.g., speed units: km/h vs. mph, custom speed limit alert thresholds, audio/vibration alarm toggles, and user body weight for calorie estimation)

Health & Calorie Disclaimer: Calorie figures presented in CycleMeter are general mathematical approximations calculated using standard cycling metabolic equivalent models (MET) and user-supplied weight. They do not constitute medical, athletic, or dietary advice.

C. Data We Do Not Collect

CycleMeter does not require user registration, logins, or social account connections. CycleMeter itself does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, physical address, or phone number
  • Your contacts, address book, or calendar entries
  • Your photos, media files, microphone audio, or camera feed
  • Your payment, banking, or credit card details

CycleMeter does not integrate with any generative AI APIs (such as Gemini API) at runtime.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the ongoing maintenance and development of CycleMeter, the application displays banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

While CycleMeter itself does not collect personal identifiers or send your GPS location to advertisers, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect, process, and use certain device information in accordance with Google's privacy policies to deliver, measure, and protect advertisements.

Data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID / GAID), vendor-specific IDs, or unique device tokens
  • Network Information: IP address, general connection type, and coarse network-derived location for regional ad serving
  • Technical Information: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings
  • Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, ad format performance metrics, and diagnostic/fraud-prevention logs

Google uses this information to deliver relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, comply with legal requirements, and calculate publisher metrics.

For more information on how Google collects, uses, and safeguards data when you use apps that integrate Google services, please consult:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all ride records, routes, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Ride Deletion: You can delete individual rides or clear ride histories directly within the application's Ride History screen. Once deleted, the corresponding local database records are permanently removed from your device.
  • Application Data Clearing: You can wipe all application data, preferences, and cached assets at any time via Android System Settings > Apps > CycleMeter > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling CycleMeter from your Android device completely removes the local database and all associated application storage maintained in the app's sandboxed directory, in accordance with Android operating system standards.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. CycleMeter leverages Android's sandboxed application architecture, which isolates the application's local SQLite database and preferences from other apps installed on the device. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage or wireless transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You are encouraged to maintain security features (such as device lock screens and current OS updates) on your mobile device.

6. Children's Privacy

CycleMeter is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us or through third-party services within the application, please contact us so that appropriate measures can be taken.
